# How to find help

If you need help, you can always reach out to us. We are here to help you with any questions you may have.

The best way to reach out to us is through our email:

[support@criminal-makers.com](mailto:support@criminal-makers.com)


# News and Updates

You will receive news from the Unity asset store notifications, but you can also follow and comment this Unity discussion thread to get the latest news and updates about the asset:

[Unity discussions](https://discussions.unity.com/t/game-event-hub-attribute-based-event-system/1586421)


# Review

Unity Asset Store ratings are very important for us, it determines the visibility of the asset and helps us to improve it. If you like the asset, please consider leaving a review on the Unity Asset Store.

[Leave a review](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/slug/303196)

> Please DO NOT use the Unity forums or asset store reviews to ask for help. We do not monitor those channels and you may not get a response. Send us an email instead.
